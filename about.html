DISCOVER OUR STORY

The Ramanyan Farm Legacy

Where nature meets royal Indian architectural splendor. Nestled in Navsari, Gujarat, Ramanyan Farm is a 14-acre private sanctuary designed to turn weddings into lifelong memories.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

A Destination Conceived For Royalty

Ramanyan Farm was created with a single uncompromising mission: to provide families with a majestic, private setting that honors the richness of traditional Indian weddings while providing contemporary 5-star amenities.

From the grand illuminated mandap pavilion to secluded palm-fringed pathways, each zone has been thoughtfully curated to provide dramatic visual backdrops for photography, royal baraat entries, pheras under starlight, and high-energy sangeet celebrations.
